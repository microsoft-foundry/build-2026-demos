__Main talking points for MAI and 1P models from MSFT:__

1. **MAI models provide frontier-level quality at a fraction of the cost**
2. **MAI models are leading:** Image-2 is the in the top 5 image models on arena.ai. Transcribe-1 offers up to 50% lower GPU cost than leading alternatives. Voice-1 Generates 60 seconds of expressive audio in under 1 second on a single GPU—one of the most efficient speech generation systems available today.
3. **Foundry has exclusive access to Microsoft AI models**—not offered on any other provider
4. **These same models power our own solutions** such as Copilot, Bing, Powerpoint, and Azure Speech
